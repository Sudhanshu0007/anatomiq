## Purpose

This document records the known limitations for the V0.1 AnatomIQ release. It ensures the release communicates scope honestly and does not overstate what the lesson can do.

## Known limitations

- The release covers only the cardiovascular MVP.
- The lesson uses a simplified major-route model.
- Not every anatomical branch or variation is shown.
- Pathology, diagnosis, and treatment are out of scope.
- The final supported environment and performance budgets may still require documented confirmation.

## Limitation rules

- Limitations should be stated plainly and without alarm.
- Do not hide limitations inside marketing language.
- Limitations should be aligned to the medical simplification record.
- The release should be honest about any areas still awaiting final validation evidence.

## Learner-facing notes

When a limitation affects the learner experience, the wording should be understandable, concise, and non-clinical.
