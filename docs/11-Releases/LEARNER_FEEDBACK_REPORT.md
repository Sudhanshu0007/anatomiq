## Purpose

This document captures learner feedback from usability or comprehension sessions. It is the place to record observations, confusion points, and improvements tied to the release evidence pack.

## Scope

### In scope

- Learner session observations.
- Comprehension findings.
- Confusion points and learner suggestions.
- Change recommendations tied to the lesson experience.

### Out of scope

- Marketing feedback.
- General product wish lists unrelated to the release scope.
- Unrecorded or anonymous assumptions about learner behavior.

## Report structure

| Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| session details | Who participated, when, and under what conditions |
| task outcomes | What learners were asked to do and what happened |
| key observations | What learners understood, hesitated on, or misunderstood |
| quotes | Brief direct statements that illustrate the finding |
| recommendations | Suggested changes linked to the lesson or UI |
| open issues | Questions needing follow-up |

## Reporting rules

- Record observations factually rather than interpretively.
- Distinguish direct participant statements from reviewer conclusions.
- Link each important finding to a relevant document, stage, or requirement when possible.
- Do not present feedback as validation by itself; it is evidence for a release decision.
