## Purpose

This document defines the brand identity for AnatomIQ. It covers the tone, personality, and visual identity rules that make the product feel recognizable and trustworthy.

## Scope

### In scope

- Brand personality.
- Identity tone and expression.
- Trust and seriousness cues.
- High-level visual direction for the product.

### Out of scope

- Final logo construction.
- Marketing campaign design.
- Asset production specifications.
- Browser-level implementation details.

## Brand identity statement

AnatomIQ is a calm, precise, and modern educational platform that helps learners understand the body through focused visual exploration.

## Brand principles

- The brand must feel educational before it feels cinematic.
- The brand must support scientific trust.
- The brand must never make the product feel childish or gimmicky.
- The brand should be polished enough to feel premium without becoming decorative.

## Brand personality

| Trait | Desired expression |
| --- | --- |
| Precise | Scientific terms, measured language, controlled visuals |
| Calm | Minimal clutter, steady pacing, no aggressive motion |
| Authoritative | Clear hierarchy, confident structure, trustworthy explanations |
| Curious | Invites exploration and inspection |
| Modern | Clean interface, contemporary layout, polished presentation |

## Brand experience

The product should feel like a guided scientific experience rather than a game, a textbook, or a generic app. The learner should feel oriented, respected, and able to inspect the body carefully.

## Brand do and do not

| Do | Do not |
| --- | --- |
| Make the body feel carefully revealed | Make the body feel like a toy or a gimmick |
| Use visual hierarchy to guide attention | Flood the interface with competing effects |
| Keep terminology consistent | Use multiple names for the same structure |
| Support a sense of scientific trust | Use exaggerated clinical drama |
| Preserve learner control | Make the brand depend on surprise or autoplay |

## Brand acceptance criteria

- [ ] The brand expresses trust, clarity, and calm exploration.
- [ ] The identity can support UI, motion, 3D, and audio decisions.
- [ ] The brand matches the educational mission.
- [ ] AI Context is current.

## Open questions

- [ ] Should the brand lean more documentary-like or more editorial and premium?
- [ ] How much visual contrast should be reserved for lesson highlights versus persistent UI?
- [ ] What level of cinematic tone is appropriate without reducing scientific seriousness?
