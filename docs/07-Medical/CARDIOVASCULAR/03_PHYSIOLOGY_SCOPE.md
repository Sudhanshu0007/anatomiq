## Purpose

This document defines the cardiovascular physiology included in the MVP. It explains which physiological ideas the lesson should teach and what depth remains out of scope.

## Scope

### In scope

- Direction of blood flow.
- Pulmonary and systemic circulation roles.
- Oxygenation change at a broad lesson level.
- Broad exchange concept at the lungs and body.

### Out of scope

- Pressure curves and detailed hemodynamics.
- Cardiac electrophysiology.
- Disease physiology and pathology.
- Microcirculation or cellular transport detail beyond the lesson objective.

## Physiology rules

- The lesson must explain why blood moves through the route, not just where it moves.
- Simplified physiology must not invert causality.
- Oxygenation change should be explained at the right level of detail for the MVP.
- The learner should understand the loop as a connected process.

## Key physiology concepts

- Blood returns from the body to the right side of the heart.
- Blood moves from the right side of the heart to the lungs.
- Blood returns from the lungs to the left side of the heart.
- Blood moves from the left side of the heart to the body.
- The route forms a loop that supports delivery and exchange.
