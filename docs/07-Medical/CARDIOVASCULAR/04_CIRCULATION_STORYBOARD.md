## Purpose

This document outlines the cardiovascular lesson storyboard. It describes the intended learning sequence before it is broken into scene-by-scene implementation details.

## Storyboard goals

- Start with whole-body orientation.
- Move through the major flow route in a legible sequence.
- Connect the route to oxygenation and exchange in a simplified but accurate way.
- End with review and summary.

## Storyboard sequence

1. Entry and lesson framing.
2. Whole-body orientation with heart and lungs visible.
3. Right-heart return from body to heart.
4. Pulmonary route from heart to lungs.
5. Lung exchange overview with simplification note.
6. Left-heart return from lungs to heart.
7. Systemic route from heart to body.
8. Knowledge check and feedback.
9. Summary and replay.

## Storyboard rules

- Each step should support one clear learner takeaway.
- The storyboard should match the route and objectives in the content package.
- Optional details should never interrupt the main story.
- The storyboard should support later implementation as scene data.
