## Purpose

This document gives the top-level overview for the cardiovascular lesson package. It explains the lesson’s educational intent, the major route, and the boundaries of the MVP cardiovascular content.

## Scope

### In scope

- Cardiovascular MVP overview.
- Major circulation route at lesson level.
- Educational framing and simplification boundaries.

### Out of scope

- Detailed valve mechanics.
- Pathology or disease lessons.
- Full vessel tree or microcirculation detail.

## Cardiovascular lesson summary

The lesson teaches the major circulation loop using a learner-controlled story that begins with whole-body orientation, moves through the right heart and lungs, then returns through the left heart to the body.

## Route summary

```text
Body -> venae cavae -> right atrium -> right ventricle -> pulmonary artery -> lungs
Lungs -> pulmonary veins -> left atrium -> left ventricle -> aorta -> body
```

## Lesson-level medical intent

- Show where the heart and lungs sit in relation to the body.
- Show how blood moves through the heart and lungs in a connected loop.
- Show the broad distinction between pulmonary and systemic circulation.
- Show how the route supports oxygenation and delivery at a simplified MVP level.

## Package guidance

- The cardiovascular lesson should remain focused on one understandable loop.
- Detail should be added only when it helps the learner understand the taught route.
- Every content choice must support the learning objectives and not just visual richness.
