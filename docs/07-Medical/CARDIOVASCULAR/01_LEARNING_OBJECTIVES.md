## Purpose

This document defines the measurable cardiovascular learning objectives for the MVP lesson. It establishes what the learner should be able to do after completing the guided experience.

## Scope

### In scope

- Cardiovascular learning outcomes.
- Objective-to-assessment alignment.
- Objective scope suitable for the MVP.

### Out of scope

- Non-cardiovascular systems.
- Clinical diagnosis or disease knowledge.
- Advanced hemodynamics beyond MVP scope.

## Learning objectives

| ID | Objective | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LO-CV-01 | Locate the heart and lungs in the lesson’s body context. | Learner identifies the structures in the orientation stage. |
| LO-CV-02 | Trace deoxygenated blood from body to lungs through the right side of the heart. | Learner orders or explains the route accurately. |
| LO-CV-03 | Trace oxygenated blood from lungs to body through the left side of the heart. | Learner orders or explains the return route accurately. |
| LO-CV-04 | Distinguish the broad roles of arteries, veins, and capillaries in the taught model. | Learner matches categories to function or route position. |
| LO-CV-05 | Explain why pulmonary and systemic circulation form a connected loop. | Learner describes the reason in learner-appropriate language. |

## Objective rules

- Each objective must be teachable in the lesson flow.
- Each objective must have at least one assessment item or review path.
- Objectives should match the approved MVP scope rather than expanding into pathology or microdetail.
